# YumSure — Ingredient and Additive Catalogue

This catalogue establishes the taxonomy, functional classification, International Numbering System (INS / E-number) references, and regulatory allergen designations under the **FSSAI (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020**.

---

## 1. Principles of Ingredient Interpretation

1. **Functional Objectivity**: Additives are not inherently toxic or harmful. They are approved food technological aids fulfilling specific functions (emulsification, shelf-life preservation, acidity regulation). YumSure explicitly states their technical function rather than generating fear-based warnings.
2. **Four-Tier Classification Model**:
   - **Identity & Name**: Canonical trade name and INS number.
   - **Technological Function**: What role the ingredient plays in food processing.
   - **Nutritional Concern**: Whether excessive consumption has clinical or dietary implications (e.g., high glycemic index, saturated fat, sodium load).
   - **Regulatory Compliance / Allergen Status**: Mandatory allergen declarations under FSSAI Clause 5(4).

---

## 2. Major FSSAI Statutory Allergens (Clause 5(4))

Foods containing any of the following 8 allergen classes must be explicitly highlighted on the label:

1. **Cereals containing gluten** (wheat, rye, barley, oats, spelt or their hybridized strains).
2. **Crustaceans** and products thereof.
3. **Eggs** and egg products.
4. **Fish** and fish products.
5. **Peanuts, Tree nuts** (almonds, walnuts, pistachios, cashews, hazelnuts, pecans, brazil nuts) and products thereof.
6. **Soybeans** and products thereof.
7. **Milk and milk products** (including lactose).
8. **Sulphite** in concentrations of 10 mg/kg or more.

## 3. Ingredient Functional Categories

### 3.1 Added Sugars & High-Potency Sweeteners

| Ingredient / Additive | INS / Synonyms | Technological Function | Nutritional Context | FSSAI Allergen? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sucrose / Table Sugar** | Sugar, Cane Sugar | Sweetening, bulk, preservation | High calorie density, high glycemic index | No |
| **Invert Sugar Syrup** | Invert Sugar | Moisture retention, crystallization inhibitor | Rapidly absorbed monosaccharides (glucose + fructose) | No |
| **High Fructose Corn Syrup** | HFCS, Corn Syrup Solids | Sweetening, shelf-life extender | High fructose load, hepatic lipogenesis risk | No |
| **Maltodextrin** | Polysaccharide | Carrier, texturizer, bulking agent | High glycemic index (~110), spikes blood glucose | No |
| **Sucralose** | INS 955 | Non-nutritive intense sweetener | 600x sweeter than sucrose, zero calorie | No |
| **Steviol Glycosides** | INS 960 | Plant-derived intense sweetener | Non-caloric natural sweetener | No |
| **Aspartame** | INS 951 | Non-nutritive sweetener | Contains phenylalanine (contraindicated in PKU) | Warning required |
| **Acesulfame Potassium** | INS 950 / Ace-K | High-intensity sweetener | Heat-stable artificial sweetener | No |
| **Sorbitol / Maltitol** | INS 420 / INS 965 | Polyol sweetener and humectant | Low glycemic, laxative effect in large doses | Caution label |

### 3.2 Refined Carbohydrates

| Ingredient | Function | Nutritional Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Refined Wheat Flour (Maida)** | Structural dough matrix, bulking | Stripped of bran and germ; low fibre, rapid digestion. |
| **Corn Starch / Maize Starch** | Thickening agent, texture binder | Pure digestible carbohydrate, elevated glycemic impact. |
| **Tapioca Starch** | Stabilizer, mouthfeel enhancer | High starch, virtually zero protein or micronutrients. |
| **White Rice Flour** | Base grain flour, crisping agent | Low fibre refined cereal. |

### 3.3 Fats and Oils

| Ingredient | Functional Role | Fatty Acid Profile & Health Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Refined Palm Oil / Palmolein** | High oxidative stability, frying medium | ~50% saturated fatty acids (palmitic acid). Stable at high frying temp. |
| **Hydrogenated Vegetable Oil (Vanaspati)** | Semi-solid fat matrix, crisp texture | Historical source of industrial trans-fats; subject to FSSAI ≤ 2% cap. |
| **Sunflower / Soybean Oil** | Liquid fat carrier, shortening | High polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFA, Omega-6 linoleic acid). |
| **Butter / Ghee (Clarified Butter)** | Rich flavour, authentic mouthfeel | High in saturated fat and dietary cholesterol. Allergen: Milk. |
| **Cocoa Butter** | Solid fat that melts at body temperature | Saturated fat (stearic and palmitic acid), low atherogenic impact of stearic. |
